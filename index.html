    RayOne® EMV

    Co-developed with Prof. Graham Barrett: Extended Visual Depth

    First and only non-diffractive extended depth of field IOL utilizing controlled positive spherical aberration to provide seamless distance, intermediate, and functional near vision without dysphotopsia.

    • Extends visual depth smoothly into computer monitor and dashboard distances while maintaining 100% photopic contrast sensitivity and zero dysphotopsia.

    • World's smallest fully preloaded 1.65mm micro-incision injector ensures a smooth, single-handed, wound-assisted planar delivery into the capsular bag.

    • Proprietary hydrophilic acrylic with 26% water equilibrium delivers pristine optical clarity with zero micro-vacuole formation over decades of clinical tracking.

    RayOne® EMV Toric

    Extended Visual Depth: Precision Corneal Astigmatism Correction

    Combines patented positive spherical aberration EDOF optics with Rayner's clinically proven closed-loop toric haptic platform, delivering uncompromised intermediate visual range and rotational stability.

    • True closed-loop haptic architecture locks into the capsular bag equator, preventing postoperative rotation and ensuring permanent cylindrical alignment.

    • Available in cylinder powers up to +6.00 D, correcting low to severe corneal astigmatism alongside presbyopic intermediate enhancement.

    • Integrated with the Raytrace® toric calculator utilizing total corneal power measurements for sub-diopter surgical accuracy and easy planning.

    RayOne Trifocal

    RayOne® Trifocal

    Patented 16-Ring Diffractive Profile: Maximum Light Transmission

    Halves the number of diffractive rings compared to conventional trifocals, reducing light scatter to only 11% and transmitting an exceptional 89% of light directly onto the retina for comfortable reading.

    • Directs 89% of available light to the retina (52% distance, 22% intermediate, 26% near) for brilliant contrast under dim evening and dining conditions.

    • Optimal near power (+3.50 D spectacle plane +2.70 D) and intermediate (+1.75 D) delivers natural smartphone reading without spectacle dependence.

    • Continuous barrier geometry blocks epithelial cell migration across the posterior capsule, driving Nd:YAG capsulotomy rates to near zero.

    RayOne® Trifocal Toric

    Full Spectacle Independence: Simultaneous Astigmatism Correction

    Offers presbyopic cataract patients with pre-existing astigmatism the ultimate trifocal optical experience, pairing patented diffractive rings with personalized toric cylinder correction.

    • Broad range of cylinder powers tailored to individual corneal measurements, neutralizing astigmatism while restoring full near-to-distance vision.

    • Resists capsular bag shrinkage and optic decentration, maintaining precise optical axis centering over the patient's visual lifetime.

    • Optimized step heights minimize pupil-dependent glares and halos during nighttime driving and low-light environments.

    RayOne Aspheric

    RayOne® Aspheric

    Aberration-Neutral Precision: Universal Corneal Compatibility

    Aberration-neutral monofocal IOL designed for pristine contrast sensitivity across all corneas, delivering crisp distance vision with unmatched optical clarity.

    • Guarantees predictable vision and heightened depth of field regardless of individual corneal curvature or previous refractive surgery.

    • Delivers 100% of light without diffractive division, maximizing visual acuity in low-contrast and specialized clinical conditions.

    • Single-handed preloaded delivery through sub-2.0mm clear corneal incisions with minimal endothelial stress and rapid self-sealing.

    Sulcoflex® Aspheric

    Pseudophakic Supplementary Platform: Reversible Ciliary Sulcus Placement

    Engineered specifically for implantation into the ciliary sulcus in pseudophakic eyes, offering a reversible solution to correct residual spherical refractive errors without explanting the primary IOL.

    • Specially contoured optic prevents pupillary capture, iris chafing, pigment dispersion syndrome, and interlenticular opacification.

    • Creates anatomical separation between primary IOL and Sulcoflex, eliminating contact and preventing hyperopic shift.

    • Smooth, wide undulating haptics distribute pressure evenly across the ciliary sulcus for stable, long-term non-inflammatory fixation.
