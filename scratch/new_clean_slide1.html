RayOne® EMV

Co-developed with Prof. Graham Barrett • Extended Visual Depth

First and only non-diffractive extended depth of field IOL utilizing controlled positive spherical aberration to provide seamless distance, intermediate, and functional near vision without dysphotopsia.

  • Extends depth of focus seamlessly into desktop computer and dashboard distance while preserving photopic contrast and eliminating halos.

  • True preloaded system pre-assembled under ISO Class 5 cleanroom conditions. Delivers the optic smoothly through sub-2.0mm incisions without manual lens touching.

  • Engineered from balanced 26% water-content hydrophilic/hydrophobic material with continuous 360° Amon-Apple square edge to eradicate glistening and prevent PCO.

RayOne® Trifocal

Halved diffractive rings for maximum light transmission

Patented 16-ring diffractive architecture halves the ring count of conventional trifocals, directing 89% of light onto the retina with lowest 11% light scatter for vibrant contrast.

  • Reduces light loss to less than half of older trifocals, providing exceptional pupil-independent brightness for night driving and dim light reading.

  • +1.75 D intermediate add for computer screens and +3.50 D near add for comfortable reading of smartphones and fine print with total spectacle independence.

  • Closed-loop corner-lock haptics guarantee zero rotational movement and exceptional axial planar stability inside the capsular bag.

RayOne® Aspheric

Aberration-neutral precision & Sulcoflex pseudophakic optics

Aberration-neutral monofocal IOL designed for pristine contrast sensitivity across all corneas, paired with Sulcoflex pseudophakic supplementary lenses for reversible post-operative refractive adjustments.

  • Zero induced spherical aberration ensures sharp, predictable vision and heightened depth of field regardless of individual corneal curvature or previous refractive surgery.

  • Reversible secondary IOL engineered for ciliary sulcus placement to correct residual cylinder, spherical surprise, or presbyopic enhancement in pseudophakic patients.

  • Ultra-smooth 1.65mm delivery eliminates incision stress and supports rapid self-sealing clear corneal wounds for speedy patient recovery.
