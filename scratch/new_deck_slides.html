ABOUT MEDIQ • CORPORATE IDENTITY

Who We Are

MEDIQ, operating as Mediq Pharma and Medical Supplies Distribution, is a Philippine-based healthcare distribution and commercial development company connecting international medical manufacturers with healthcare professionals, hospitals, surgical centers, and institutions across the country.

Established in specialized ophthalmic technologies, MEDIQ aims to build a diversified healthcare portfolio while retaining its commitment to product quality, technical understanding, responsive service, and sustainable partnerships.

Precision Optics

Our Vision

To become one of the Philippines' most trusted and forward-thinking healthcare solutions partners, advancing the accessibility, quality, and delivery of care through innovative products, responsible commercialization, and enduring partnerships.

Our Mission

To bridge global healthcare innovation with the evolving needs of the Philippine market by sourcing, marketing, promoting, selling, and distributing quality medical products and technologies, while providing healthcare professionals with reliable support, education, and service.
