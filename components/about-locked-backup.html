WHO WE ARE

We believe in meaningful clinical innovation

Operating as Mediq Pharma and Medical Supplies Distribution, we connect international medical manufacturers with ophthalmologists, hospitals, surgical centers, and healthcare institutions across the Philippines.

Curious, meticulous, and committed, we do far more than distribute medical devices. We combine cleanroom-grade cold-chain logistics, regulatory stewardship, and hands-on surgical education so every clinic and patient receives world-class ophthalmic care.
